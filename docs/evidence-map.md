# Evidence Map — claim-to-source ledger

> Internal transparency record. These tags are intentionally kept out of the polished README; they live here so every published claim can be traced. Rule: a claim is only as strong as its weakest tag.

## Legend

* `[CV]` — stated in the candidate's CV as career history. Accepted as declared professional trajectory; not verifiable in code.
* `[EVIDENCE: REPO]` — observed directly in the private versioned lab repository (files, commit history, docs). Never reproduced verbatim in public; only abstracted as patterns.
* `[NIVORA]` — declared lab activity (2022–Present per CV), corroborated indirectly by lab output. Describes initiative scope, not a company.
* `[INFERENCE]` — reasonable professional inference, stated as such. Must not be presented as fact.
* `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION]` — blocked until the candidate confirms. Excluded from public claims meanwhile.
* `[NOT VERIFIED]` — no evidence available. Must not appear in the portfolio.
