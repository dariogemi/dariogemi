# Non-Goals — what this portfolio deliberately does NOT do

1. **Not a smart-home showcase.** Home automation is the lab bench, never the headline. No YAML dumps, no device tours, no household detail.
2. **No borrowed authority.** Third-party integrations and external components are never presented as personal authorship. Any component with unconfirmed authorship is excluded entirely pending confirmation.
3. **No inflated AI claims.** No RAG, fine-tuning, eval scores, SLO statistics, or throughput numbers are claimed. Each case study carries an explicit Limitations section.
4. **No rankings or scores.** No "top %", no 10/10 skill bars, no "world-class". Seniority is expressed through scope words (led, designed, delivered) tied to dated roles.
5. **No private data.** No IPs, MACs, device IDs, keys, tokens, credentials, topology, routines, locations, schedules, or personal documents — in content or in git history.
6. **No company inflation.** Nivora is described strictly as an independent lab initiative, never as a staffed company, funded startup, consultancy, or commercial product.
7. **No developer misclassification bait.** Hands-on engineering (Python, Bash, SQL) is shown as evidence of architecture capability, while the headline positioning stays Senior Technical BA / Product & Project.
8. **No keyword stuffing.** Discoverability terms are integrated into real sentences about real work; the keyword list exists once, in `zorin-profile.md`.
